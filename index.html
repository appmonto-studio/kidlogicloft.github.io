Our principles

Designed for a parent’s peace of mind.

Play stays inside the product. The promises around it stay plain and specific.

Private by default

No accounts, analytics, behavioural profiles or personal data collection.

No commercial pressure

No ads, subscriptions, timers or manufactured urgency. Optional purchases are clearly described and kept behind a parent gate.

Works without a connection

Core play is available offline, without sign-in or a network connection.
